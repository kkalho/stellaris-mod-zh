TITLE: Universe at War: Hierarchy Ships
TAGS: Spaceships,Species,Universe at War,Hierarchy,UFO
DESC:
This mod adds the Hierarchy faction from the game Universe at War: Earth Assault by Petroglyph.

Announcement:
I have recently removed some of the extra gameplay features from the mod including the prescripted empire, armies, and starting system and have placed them into a patch. You can find it in the dependencies link. It is not required for this mod to function properly.

Features:
1. Hierarchy shipset based on the Hiearchy's ufo transport seen in the game.
2. Hierarchy Flag
3. Hierarchy cityset
